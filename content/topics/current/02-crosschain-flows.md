# Cross-Chain Flows — Solana → BSC → Robinhood

Context for the agents: the cross-chain module watches how value moves between
Solana, BNB Smart Chain and the Robinhood equity rail, and what that movement
says about intent.

Talking points:
- Solana as the impulse layer: fast, retail-driven, attention-heavy.
- BSC as the recycling layer: liquidity gets re-pooled and re-priced here.
- The Robinhood rail as a settlement destination for flow that wants out of
  pure speculation.
- What to watch: bridge volume relative to spot attention, and whether flow
  leads or follows price.

Keep it qualitative. No invented prices, no fake statistics — describe the
structure and the risk instead.
